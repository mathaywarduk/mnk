It's strange how often we have a fond feeling toward a place when we didn't fully enjoy the time we spent there. Nice to know that it's the good feelings that stick with us.

We stayed in Glenelg for a week in January, to test out working from Scotland for the first time. It was cold, there was a storm and the locals asked us why the fuck we were there. But it was beautiful.

Kerry swam in the sea a couple of times, which must have been freezing. Rudi got to play on his favourite beach and Mat beat the local barman at pool.

What more do we need?
