We'd sold Frog Lane, so the move to Scotland was on. Time to try out living and working from there, so we booked 2 weeks of working followed by a week's holiday.


## Starting at Pierhouse Hotel

It was Mat's birthday, so we **had** to go to the Pierhouse Hotel to eat seafood, drink whisky and swim in the sea.

{% include gallery.html id="pierhouse" %}

## Furnace

After nearly getting stuck in traffic that would have lasted hours, we arrived Ploughamn's Cottage, just up the hill behind Furnace.

Walking in, it smelt amazing, and the decor was beautiful. The hot tub didn't work, but we had a cabin porch and orange wine, so we were just fine.

{% include gallery.html id="furnace" %}

## Bruach 

Our first week in Furnace was nice, but there wasn't a lot there. We felt much better in Balquhidder - a place we knew well.

Bruach itself wasn't our style, but it's position between the loch and the forest was perfect for evening swims and lunchtime walks.

{% include gallery.html id="bruach" %}

## Time to relax

Although we were in Scotland, we hadn't actually had a real break from work since Christmas. A week to just explore, mostly in the van, was required.

### Monachyle 10K

Kerry had done the 10K race at Monachyle last year and, as we were just up the glen, it would have been rude not to do it again.

She smashed her PB and, after they escaped, Mat got a close encounter with the Monachyle goats.

{% include gallery.html id="probation-monachyle" %}

### Three munros near Glen Shiel

After Monachyle we drove up north and spent a couple of days in Glen Shiel. First at the hotel, where we were entertained by Jordan, the chef from the nearby diner, while the hoped the chef cooking our dinner would reciver from his breakdown...

Thankfully he did, and the next day we woke up to a herd of goats making their way through the hotel grounds, before we went to climb three munros. And smashed it.

{% include gallery.html id="three-munros" %}

A night at Glenshiel campsite was followed by time in Torridon, near Ardfern and on the Isle of Gigha.
