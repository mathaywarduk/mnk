We had sold Frog Lane, so the move to Scotland was real. Before committing fully, we decided to try living and working there. Two weeks working, followed by a week off.

## Starting at Pierhouse Hotel

It was Mat’s birthday, so we had to go to the Pierhouse Hotel. Seafood, whisky and swimming in the sea felt like the right way to start.

{% include gallery.html id="pierhouse" %}

## Furnace

After narrowly avoiding traffic that would have trapped us for hours, we arrived at Ploughman’s Cottage just above Furnace.

It smelt amazing when we walked in and the decor was beautiful. The hot tub did not work, but we had a porch, orange wine and no complaints.

{% include gallery.html id="furnace" %}

## Bruach

Our first week in Furnace was calm but quiet. Balquhidder felt better. Somewhere familiar.

Bruach was not quite our style, but its position between the loch and the forest was perfect for evening swims and lunchtime walks.

{% include gallery.html id="bruach" %}

## Time to relax

Although we were in Scotland, we had not really stopped working since Christmas. A proper break was needed.

### Monachyle 10K

Kerry had run the Monachyle 10K the year before and, staying just up the glen, it would have been rude not to do it again.

She smashed her PB. After a brief escape, Mat had a close encounter with the Monachyle goats.

{% include gallery.html id="probation-monachyle" %}

### Three munros near Glen Shiel

From Monachyle we headed north to Glen Shiel. Dinner came with entertainment from Jordan, the chef from the nearby diner, while we all quietly hoped the chef cooking our food would recover from his breakdown.

Thankfully he did.

We woke the next morning to goats wandering through the hotel grounds, then headed out to climb three munros. And smashed it.

{% include gallery.html id="three-munros" %}

A night at Glenshiel campsite was followed by time in Torridon, near Ardfern and on the Isle of Gigha.
