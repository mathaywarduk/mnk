For the first time, we decided to have Christmas as just the three of us, and drove up to Scotland to spend some time in our favourite city.

## Edinburgh

We booked a place in Dean Village, which was quirky and small but perfect for us. A day walking to Leith and trying out as many of the natural wine bars as we could felt like a great idea until Kerry's hangover the next day.

## Christmas eve wild camp

After Edinburgh, we spent a couple of nights in Frances' Sketch pad (the less said about that the better), before finding a spot for a wild camp on Christmas eve. After a bit if debate, we fell back on an old favourite and woke up on Christmas day by Old Castle Lachlan.

Kerry ws brave enough to go for a swim. Mat just enjoyed the bacon sandwich.
