3 weeks staying in holiday cottages in Scotland, without exploring, just felt weird. Luckily we had a week booked with no real plans.

We started by finding a glen behind the hills near Ullapool on the route to Loch Achall. Rudi loved playing in the river there.

## Torridon hills

We had thought about tackling An Teallach, but being shattered, decided to skip climbing unil the next day. As we had booked the stables at Torridon, we found a munro on the way... Maol Chean-dearg.

A scary bridge over the river and a pretty cool bothy made the otherwise boggy walk in a bit more interesting. Then we really struggled our way up to the top.

The last part, climbing up the boulder field, was actually pretty fun and we got views of Beinn Damh, which we'd climbed last year. The drinks in the whisky bar at the Torridon felt like just reward.

## Back to Monachyle

Having stayed in the Pilot Panther a few times, we tried one of Monachyle's other glamping setups and booked in at the Ferry Waiting Room. Walking through the trees to find Rudi waiting on the deck was the best!

{% include gallery.html id="ferry-waiting" %}

## Arran again

We ended our little break with a trip back to Arran. The Lagg Inn was a bit old fashioned, but the food was good and the locals were nice, particularly the four-legged ones like Briagh ❤️

We got ourselves up Goatfell on the non-tourist route... incredible views.
