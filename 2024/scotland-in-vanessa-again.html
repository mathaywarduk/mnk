Scotland in Vanessa, again

Scotland

April 2024

It was Mat’s birthday, so we planned a trip to Scotland, mostly in the North West.

We drove up on the Friday evening, arriving at Three Lochs Forest Drive at about midnight.

Lochinver & Quinag

Our first proper stop was Lochinver. We’d booked a cabin with hot tub views of Suilven and had dinner at Delilah’s. Last time we visited was during Covid, so it was nice to actually be inside and warm.

Sunday was Mat’s birthday and he wanted to climb the three peaks of Quinag. We set off just after 7am and started the walk soon after.

Two peaks in, the wind started picking up and annoying the hell out of us, but we persevered, even when the wind pushed us around. We were 100m or so from final summit when the hail started and the visibility dropped. After sheltering behind a rock for 5 minutes, we decided to call it a day. The right decision.

Clashnessie beach

After the drama the day before, we opted for a more relaxed day and went to the beach. Rudi, as always, loved playing with his toy on the shore and Mat got some lovely pictures of him and Kerry playing.

Down south to Torridon tomorrow.

“Let’s climb that one!”

We hadn’t stopped in the Torridon campsite before because it was always so full, but that night we got it to ourselves and watched the sun setting on the top of Liathach.

The next morning, we wanted to climb again. Kerry chose one from the view out of the van window; Beinn Damh.

We’d tried it before with Howard and Sarah and got rained off, but this time the weather was glorious.

It was a bit airy in places, but Mat even managed to book a meal at a Michelin restaurant on the way up. Such a multi-tasker!

After the climb, we needed showers and, luckily, we were booked in at Sheildaig campsite for a couple of days. From there we enjoyed the food at the pub, bought some art from a local painter, and had a walk around the loch at the Torridon estate.

Return to Glenelg

On the way back down south we booked in at the campsite near Glenelg, but fancied a drink first. Mat dropped Kerry in Glenelg and she ran the 5k to the brewery, meeting him there.

While she was running, Mat had met the brewery dog and smashed through a pint already.

Off to the campsite, where Kerry recovered with a dip in the river.

Travelling south again, we stopped at our favourite spot on Loch Linnhe for lunch.
